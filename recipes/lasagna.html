Lasagna recipe

Lasagna is a rich, savory Italian dish made by layering flat pasta sheets with a hearty meat sauce, a creamy white sauce or cheese filling, and melted toppings.

Ingredients You'll Need for Lasagna

  • Meat: This super meaty lasagna has sweet Italian sausage and lean ground beef.
  • Onion and garlic: An onion and two cloves of garlic are cooked with the meat to add tons of flavor.
  • Tomato products: You'll need a can of crushed tomatoes, two cans of tomato sauce, and two cans of tomato paste.
  • Sugar: Two tablespoons of white sugar add subtle sweetness and enhance the flavor of the sauce.
  • Spices and seasonings: This lasagna recipe is flavored with fresh parsley, dried basil leaves, salt, Italian seasoning, fennel seeds, and black pepper.
  • Lasagna noodles: Use store-bought or homemade lasagna noodles.
  • Cheeses: Parmesan, mozzarella, and ricotta cheese make this lasagna extra decadent.
  • Egg: An egg helps bind the ricotta so it doesn't ooze out of the lasagna when you cut into it.

Recipe

The Meat Sauce (Bolognese): Ground beef or sausage is simmered with onions, garlic, crushed tomatoes, and herbs to create a thick, flavorful red sauce.The Cheese/Cream Layer: Traditional Italian recipes use a silky Béchamel sauce (made from butter, flour, and milk). Many popular variations swap this for a mixture of ricotta cheese, egg, and parsley.The Assembly: Alternating layers of cooked lasagna noodles, meat sauce, and the cream/cheese mixture are built inside a deep baking dish.The Baking: The top is covered with a generous layer of mozzarella and parmesan cheese. It is baked in the oven until the pasta is tender, the sauce is bubbling, and the cheese turn golden brown.
